How to stay off the supper menu

This is real-time combat with manual aiming. The camera follows your chicken. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, then hold the mouse button or Space to fire. Clicking the arena captures the mouse when supported; Escape releases it and pauses. Without capture, move the pointer inside the arena to aim. Keyboard-only: Q / E or left / right arrows rotate the camera, up / down arrows adjust aim height, and Space fires.

Your hen turns around and shoots eggs directly from its rear, then turns back. Shots travel where the reticle points: lead a moving Grandma. The orange circle marks her next grab. Move out or press Shift to dodge with brief invulnerability. When she misses and catches her breath, hits deal double damage. Dodge recharges in 1.3 seconds; there are no rapid-click requirements.

Grandma gets faster across ten encounters. Escape equipment gives more hearts and shot strength. The first win in each encounter grants eggs once. Getting caught never removes farm progress. Returning to the farm, switching tabs, or losing window focus pauses combat. Reloaded encounters wait for you to resume; your farm still earns while the page is open.
