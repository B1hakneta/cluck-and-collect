How to stay off the supper menu

The amber lane is where Grandma will reach after your next move. Waddle to safety to charge a double-strength egg. Turn around and shoot an egg from your rear when your lane is safe, or catch your breath to recover a heart. Later encounters use two-handed sweeps. There is always a safe lane.

Nothing moves until you choose. Use Tab and Enter/Space, or A / D to waddle, F to fire, and R to rest while the arena is focused. Eggs are free in encounters. Getting caught never costs farm eggs or purchases. Returning to the farm pauses the encounter, and your turn is saved.

Unlock escape equipment in the farm shop for more hearts and stronger eggs. Every ten farm levels also adds one egg strength.
